      Saving to your API

      Saved on this phone

      Everything is saved on this phone first, then to your API. The key stays on this device only. Use the same address and key on your partner's phone and you share one list.

      How the numbers work
      1. In your account = start balance + money in − money out.
      2. List items, this month's debt payments and planned savings are still to pay until you tick them.
      3. Free to spend = in your account − still to pay. Split over the days left, that's what you can spend each day.
      4. If your bank shows something different, tap Update. The difference is saved so the sums always match.
      5. At month end, bills and groceries marked every month come back by themselves.
